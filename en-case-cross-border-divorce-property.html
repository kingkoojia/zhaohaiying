Cross-Border Divorce and Asset Investigation: Court Orders and Hidden Property

International Family Case Updated: August 12, 2026

In an international divorce, the real difficulty is often not whether the marriage ends, but whether all assets can be found and whether what is found can actually be divided. Hidden accounts, transferred equity, and unreported insurance can be investigated item by item through court investigation orders; under Chinese law, the at-fault spouse should receive a smaller share. This article follows the anonymized case of a China-Germany couple.

1. Case Background

Mr. Chen (Chinese national) and Ms. Tang (German national) had been married for years and had one daughter. After living separately in two countries for a long time, Mr. Chen filed for divorce.

Mr. ChenPlaintiffChinese national; held multiple properties, company equity, and bank accounts

DaughterChildGerman national; lived with her mother in Germany

Disputed issues: divorce, child custody and support, division of community property, and compensation for marital fault.

2. Key Difficulties

  1. Scattered assets: real property, company equity, multiple bank accounts, wealth management products, insurance, housing provident fund, and pensions were held at many institutions;
  2. Incomplete disclosure: the other party's asset disclosure was incomplete; several accounts, policies, and wealth management products were not reported;
  3. Equity transfer: equity was transferred to a related company during the marriage;
  4. Cross-border evidence: overseas passports, birth certificates, and chat records required notarization, authentication, and translation;
  5. Support standard: because the daughter lived in Germany, child support had to reflect German living standards.

3. Case Process

Step 1: Prepare identity and overseas evidence

  1. Completed notarization, authentication, and translation of the daughter's birth certificate, passport, and Ms. Tang's identity documents;
  2. Notarized the power of attorney in Germany with translations;
  3. Notarized overseas chat records and text messages through a German notary and attached translations.

Step 2: Asset disclosure and court investigation

  1. The court issued the community property disclosure notice, forms, and undertaking;
  2. Submitted agency opinions, defense submissions, and evidence challenge opinions on the asset disclosure;
  3. Applied for multiple court investigation orders covering Bank of Communications, MYbank, Bank of Shanghai, Agricultural Bank of China, Shengjing Bank, Tenpay (WeChat Pay), Alipay, the tax authority, the housing provident fund center, the vehicle administration, securities clearing institutions, and insurance companies;
  4. Applied for valuation of the company equity and real property.

Step 3: Mediation and trial

  1. Submitted property division plans with calculations under different division ratios;
  2. Drafted a divorce mediation agreement under which each party kept assets in their own name and the other party paid a lump-sum property division amount and child support;
  3. Simultaneously argued for a reduced share for the at-fault spouse, back child support, and compensation.

4. Legal Analysis

4.1 Investigation orders are the key to finding assets

Situation: one spouse conceals bank deposits, wealth management products, insurance, and WeChat/Alipay funds.

Risk: without complete asset leads, it is difficult to claim division after the divorce is final.

Recommendation: apply for multiple court investigation orders covering banks, payment tools, securities, insurance, tax, housing provident fund, vehicles, and real property; if equity was transferred, ask the court to investigate the related company.

4.2 A spouse who conceals or transfers assets should receive less

Situation: equity was transferred to a related company and accounts were omitted during the marriage.

Risk: without evidence, the assets are divided under the ordinary rules.

Recommendation: under Article 1092 of the Civil Code of the People's Republic of China, argue that a spouse who conceals, transfers, or squanders community property should receive a smaller share or none.

4.3 Notarization and authentication of cross-border evidence

Situation: overseas passports, birth certificates, and chat records must be submitted to a Chinese court.

Risk: evidence that is not notarized, authenticated, and translated is not admitted.

Recommendation: follow the notarization + authentication + court-recognized translation chain; have overseas chat records notarized by an overseas notary and attach translations.

5. Recommendations for Clients

  1. Lock down asset leads as early as possible: keep account numbers, policy numbers, and transfer records;
  2. Make investigation orders comprehensive: banks, Alipay/WeChat, securities, insurance, housing provident fund, and pensions should all be covered;
  3. Start overseas evidence authentication early to avoid delaying the hearing;
  4. Prepare both mediation and trial tracks with complete calculations of the minimum divisible property, child support, and compensation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1: What if the other party refuses to provide bank statements?

A: You can apply to the court for investigation orders, and the lawyer may use them to obtain records directly from banks, payment platforms, and securities companies without the other party's cooperation.

Q2: Can equity transferred to another company still be divided?

A: Yes. Through investigation orders, the equity structure of the related company can be identified. Equity acquired during the marriage is community property, and the transfer cannot defeat a lawful division.

Q3: Can overseas chat records be used as evidence?

A: Yes, but they must be notarized by an overseas notary institution, translated into Chinese, and supported by a complete authentication chain before the court will admit them.

Q4: How is child support determined when the child lives abroad?

A: The court considers the actual cost of living in the child's place of residence, the parents' income, and the child's needs. Local living cost data can be submitted as reference.
